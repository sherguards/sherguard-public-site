How Payouts Work

SherGuard uses a manual review process.
1. Earn commission Your referred customer becomes a paid SherGuard subscriber and commission is created.
2. Commission is reviewed SherGuard reviews refunds, chargebacks, fraud signals, and referral quality before approving commission.
3. Reach $50 approved commission Once your approved available balance reaches $50, the Request Payout button becomes available.
4. Request your payout SherGuard receives your payout request in the Affiliate Admin payout queue.
5. SherGuard reviews and pays The owner verifies the payout details, approves the request, sends the payment manually, and records the payment.
6. Payout becomes Paid After payment is actually sent, SherGuard marks the request as paid and the payout appears in your history.
