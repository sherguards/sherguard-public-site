DataDome and SherGuard both address modern online fraud and automated abuse, but they approach business protection from different product perspectives. This comparison examines bot detection, fake signup protection, device risk, API abuse, payment fraud, account protection, mobile apps, SaaS platforms, marketplaces, fintech, e-commerce, and AI-powered attacks.

Introduction

DataDome and SherGuard target the same growing fraud problem from different angles

Online businesses are facing a new generation of automated fraud. Attackers are no longer limited to simple scripts. They can use bot networks, compromised accounts, stolen credentials, automated signup systems, disposable email addresses, device farms, API automation, scraping tools, and increasingly sophisticated AI-driven activity.

The result is a problem that reaches far beyond traditional cybersecurity. A fraudulent signup can become an abusive account. An abusive account can attack an API. An automated account can eventually attempt payment fraud. A stolen credential can lead to account takeover and unauthorized transactions.

DataDome is a major cyberfraud protection provider focused on websites, mobile apps, APIs, automated traffic, account protection, and fraud prevention. Its current product portfolio includes Bot Protect, Account Protect, API Protection, DDoS Protect, and additional capabilities for AI-agent traffic. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

SherGuard takes a more focused trust-intelligence approach. Its core protection model brings five business risks together: Fake Signup Detection, Device Risk Intelligence, Bot Detection, API Abuse Detection, and Payment Fraud Detection.

The important question is therefore not simply which platform has more features. The better question is which approach matches the security problems a particular business is trying to solve.
