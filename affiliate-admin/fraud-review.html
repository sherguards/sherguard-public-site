Risk Signals

What SherGuard should review
Self-referrals Affiliate and customer share email, device, payment method, IP pattern, or organization identity.
Fake traffic Unusual click spikes, low-quality traffic, bot-like browsing, or repeated visitor patterns.

Duplicate accounts Multiple organizations created from the same user, browser, IP, or payout identity.

Review Outcome

Recommended action model
Approve Legitimate referral, active paid customer, no suspicious fraud signals.
Hold Needs more time because payment, refund, or identity risk is unclear.
