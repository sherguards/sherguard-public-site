Questions

Pricing FAQ

Do full plans include all modules?

Yes. Every full platform plan includes Email Risk, Device Risk, Bot Detection, API Abuse, and Payment Fraud Intelligence.

What changes between plans?

Daily checks, API keys, team seats, support level, and operational limits increase by plan.

Is AI Agent Permissions sold separately?

No. It is a governance and settings layer included with the platform, not a separate detection module.

Can I start with one module?

Yes. You can subscribe to an individual module and upgrade later to a full platform plan.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Subscriptions are managed through the billing flow, and cancellation access is available through customer billing management.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan lets customers test all five modules with daily usage limits before upgrading.

Do I need a developer?

You can test manually in the dashboard. API integration requires a developer or technical team.

What happens after upgrade?

After payment, the subscription flow updates the plan, increases limits, and unlocks the plan capacity automatically.
