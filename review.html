Review Guidelines

Before You Leave A Review

We value honest, authentic, and constructive feedback from customers and partners.

Use SherGuard First

Please leave a review only after you've personally used SherGuard and experienced the platform.

Share Your Honest Experience

Whether your experience is positive or includes suggestions for improvement, your honest feedback helps us continue building a better platform.

Protect Sensitive Information

Constructive Feedback Is Welcome

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Reviews

Who can review SherGuard?

Anyone who has personally used SherGuard, including customers, developers, business owners, and approved SherGuard Partners.

Can affiliates leave a review?

Yes. If you've personally used SherGuard and are familiar with the platform, you're welcome to share your honest experience.

Does SherGuard reward reviews?

No. We never pay for reviews or offer incentives in exchange for positive feedback. We value authentic, unbiased opinions.

Where should I ask questions?

If you need assistance before leaving a review, contact our team at support@sherguard.com and we'll be happy to help.
